## 2. What Information We Collect

When you sign in with your Google account, we collect:

- **Your name** (from your Google profile)
- **Your email address** (from your Google profile)
- **Your profile picture URL** (from your Google profile, if available)
- **Task progress** (which tasks you have marked as complete)
- **Dates you enter** (start date and planned travel date)
- **Timestamps** (when you signed up, when you last used the tracker)

We do **not** collect:
- Passwords (Google handles authentication)
- Payment information
- Location data
- Contact lists
- Any other personal data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

- Provide the Qatar Job Hunt Tracker service
- Show your personal progress on your dashboard
- Allow Visa Aylin Consultancy staff to monitor your progress and provide guidance
- Send you deadline reminders and career support (via WhatsApp when you choose to share)
- Improve our services

## 4. Who Can See Your Data

- **You:** Full access to your own data
- **Visa Aylin Consultancy admins:** Can see your name, email, progress percentage, and task completion status to provide consultancy services
- **Other users:** Cannot see your data
- **Third parties:** We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties

## 5. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored securely on **Google Firebase** (Firestore) servers in Europe. Google Firebase complies with major international data protection standards including GDPR.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data at any time (visible in the app)
- **Correct** your data (via the "Update Info" button)
- **Delete** your data — contact us at seridifac@outlook.fr and we will delete your account within 7 days
- **Export** your data — contact us to receive a copy

## 7. Cookies & Tracking

The app uses browser storage to keep you signed in (managed by Google Firebase). We do not use tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising cookies.

## 8. Age Restriction

This service is intended for users **18 years or older**. If you are under 18, please do not use this service.
